FOREWORD
Welcome to the second season of the Centre Court Pickleball League – Hyderabad (CCPL-
HYD).
CCPL was created with a simple ambition—to redefine how pickleball is played,
experienced and celebrated in Hyderabad.
The league exists to provide players with a professional platform, franchise owners with a
meaningful sporting property, partners with an authentic community, and fans with a
competition they can truly belong to.
Unlike traditional tournaments, CCPL is built around franchises, player contracts,
auctions, team identities and long-term relationships. Every player represents more than
themselves. Every franchise represents more than a weekend of competition.
This handbook serves as the official governing document of the league. It establishes the
rules, procedures and operational standards applicable to every franchise owner, player,
captain, official and league representative participating in CCPL Season 2.
Participation in the league constitutes acceptance of these regulations.
The League Committee reserves the right to interpret, amend or supplement these
regulations where necessary to ensure the integrity, fairness and smooth conduct of the
competition.

1. LEAGUE PHILOSOPHY
1.1 About CCPL
The Centre Court Pickleball League (CCPL-HYD) is Hyderabad's premier franchise-based
pickleball league.
The league has been established to create a sustainable, professional and commercially
viable ecosystem for the sport by bringing together players, franchise owners, brands and
fans under one competitive platform.
CCPL is designed to reward excellence on and off the court by combining elite competition
with entertainment, storytelling, community engagement and professional league
operations.
The league aims to become India's benchmark franchise pickleball competition.
1.2 Vision
To build India's most respected city-based pickleball league by developing athletes,
empowering franchise ownership and creating unforgettable sporting experiences.
1.3 Mission
CCPL shall strive to:
• Elevate competitive pickleball through professional league operations.
• Create meaningful commercial opportunities for franchise owners and partners.
• Build long-term player careers through structured contracts and league participation.
• Deliver world-class experiences for spectators and digital audiences.

2. LEAGUE STRUCTURE
2.1 Competition Format
CCPL Season 2 shall consist of Eight (8) franchise teams competing in a single-season
championship.
Each franchise shall be owned and operated by an approved ownership group recognised
by the League Committee.
2.2 League Format
The competition shall be conducted in three phases.
Phase One
League Stage
The League Stage shall be conducted in a single Round Robin format.
Each franchise shall compete against every other franchise once during the League Stage.
The league stage will be conducted over seven matchdays, with all teams competing on
each matchday.
Each completed Battle shall contribute towards the League Table.
Phase Two
Playoffs
The top four franchises, determined by league standings, shall qualify for the Playoffs.
Playoff structure shall be announced before commencement of the season.

9. BATTLE REGULATIONS
9.1 Definition
A Battle is the official contest between two (2) franchises in the Centre Court Pickleball
League – Hyderabad.
Each Battle shall consist of six (6) scheduled matches played in a prescribed order. Where
both franchises win three (3) matches each, a deciding contest known as the
GameBreaker shall determine the winner of the Battle.
9.2 Order of Play
Every Battle shall be contested in the following order:
Match 	Discipline
Match 1 	Men's Doubles I
Match 2 	Women's Doubles
Match 3 	Men's Singles
Match 4 	Women's Singles
Match 5 	Men's Doubles II
Match 6 	Mixed Doubles
If the Battle is tied after the completion of the six scheduled matches, a tiebreaker, i.e.
GameBreaker shall be played to determine the winner.

© CENTRE COURT SPORTS AND ENTERTAINMENT, 2026
9.3 Match Format
All scheduled matches shall be played using the following format:
• 	All Singles and Doubles matches shall be played as a single set to 15 points.
• 	Service Point Scoring shall be used. A point may only be scored by the serving
player/team.
• 	If the serving player/team wins a rally, it scores one point and continues serving.
• 	If the receiving player/team wins a rally, no point is awarded. Service passes to the
receiving player/team.
• 	Singles: Service shall alternate between players in accordance with standard
pickleball service rules.
• 	Doubles: Both players on a team shall serve before service passes to the opposing
team, except at the start of the game where only one player serves first, in
accordance with standard pickleball rules.
• 	Standard pickleball rules governing service sequence, server position, receiver
position, faults, and the non-volley zone (kitchen) shall apply.
• 	A game is won by the first player/team to reach 15 points, provided it leads by at
least two (2) points.
• 	If the score reaches 14–14, the game enters deuce.
• 	From deuce onwards, play shall continue until one player/team establishes a two-
point lead (e.g., 16–14, 17–15, 22–20).
• 	There shall be no maximum point cap; play shall continue until the required two-
point margin is achieved.
• 	The Referee's decision on scoring, service order, and rule interpretation shall be
final.
9.4 Time-Outs
Each franchise shall be entitled to one (1) Team Time-Out during each scheduled match
of a Battle.
The following conditions shall apply:
• 	Each Time-Out shall have a maximum duration of sixty (60) seconds.

© CENTRE COURT SPORTS AND ENTERTAINMENT, 2026
• 	Only the Team Captain or the player(s) currently participating in the match may
request a Time-Out.
• 	A Time-Out may only be requested when play is dead and before the next serve has
commenced.
• 	The Referee shall have sole authority to grant and resume play following a Time-
Out.
• 	Unused Time-Outs shall not carry forward to subsequent matches.
Each scheduled match shall have a separate Time-Out allocation.
9.4.1 Medical Time-Out
• 	A Medical Time-Out may be granted in the event of an injury sustained during a
match.
• 	The Referee shall initially assess the situation and may suspend play for a
reasonable period to allow the injured player to receive medical attention.
• 	The duration of the Medical Time-Out shall be determined by the Referee in
consultation with the Tournament Director, who may also seek the guidance of the
League Committee where necessary.
• 	If the player is deemed unable to resume play within the allotted time, or if
continuing the match is considered unsafe or impractical, the Tournament Director,
in consultation with the League Committee, shall determine the outcome of the
match in accordance with the League Regulations.
• 	All such decisions shall be final and binding.
Each scheduled match shall have a separate Time-Out allocation.
9.5 Battle Winner
A franchise shall be declared the winner of the Battle upon winning four (4) or more
scheduled matches.
Where the result stands at three (3) matches each, the Battle shall proceed immediately to
the GameBreaker.

11. GAMEBREAKER REGULATIONS
11.1 Purpose
The GameBreaker is the official tie-breaking mechanism of the Centre Court Pickleball
League – Hyderabad.
It has been designed to reward squad depth, tactical planning and overall team strength by
requiring participation from multiple players rather than relying on a single individual or
doubles pairing.
The GameBreaker shall be played only where a Battle is tied after the completion of the six
(6) scheduled matches.
11.2 Match Format
The GameBreaker shall be contested as a singles match using:

• 	First team to score twenty-five (25) points.
• 	Teams shall not be required to win by a margin of two (2) points.
• 	The first team to reach twenty-five (25) points shall be declared the winner.
11.3 Team Composition
Each franchise shall submit a GameBreaker Rotation consisting of eight (8) players.
The submitted rotation shall be part of the Official Battle Line-Up.
The submitted order shall remain confidential until both franchises have completed line-

Every franchise shall nominate exactly eight (8) players for the GameBreaker.

• 	deduction of League Points;
• 	forfeiture of the Battle; or
• 	any other disciplinary sanction determined by the League Committee.
11.5 Rotation Order
The eight nominated players shall be listed in a fixed playing order.
Each player shall participate for one block of four (4) consecutive rally points.
The rotation shall continue sequentially until all eight players have participated.

ownership and player participation as two distinct and independent roles.
Ownership represents stewardship, investment and leadership, while player participation
is intended to be earned through the League's established player acquisition mechanisms.
Accordingly, the League's preferred position is that franchise owners shall ordinarily
remain non-playing stakeholders.

15.2 Guiding Principles
In administering this Chapter, the League Committee shall be guided by the following
principles:
• 	To preserve the integrity of the Player Acquisition system.
• 	To maximise opportunities for the wider player community.
• 	To ensure franchise ownership is not treated as a pathway to playing participation.
• 	To act in the long-term sporting and commercial interests of the League.
15.3 No Automatic Right to Participate
Ownership of a franchise shall not, by itself, confer any right or preference to participate as
a player in the League.
Similarly, participation as a player shall not create any preferential pathway to franchise
ownership or any future owner participation mechanism.
The League shall not adopt or recognise any arrangement that encourages players to
acquire, directly or indirectly, an ownership interest in a franchise solely for the purpose of
obtaining a playing opportunity.
15.4 Substance Over Form
The League Committee shall determine ownership based on the substance of an
ownership arrangement rather than its legal form.
In doing so, the League Committee may consider, amongst other things:
• 	beneficial ownership;
• 	financial interest;
• 	management or operational control;
• 	decision-making authority;
• 	historical ownership arrangements; and
• 	any other information considered relevant.

The League Committee may disregard any ownership structure or arrangement where it
reasonably believes that such arrangement has been created, modified or maintained
primarily to circumvent these Regulations or obtain an unfair sporting advantage.
15.5 Anti-Circumvention
No franchise or individual shall create, transfer, restructure or otherwise alter the
ownership or control of a franchise for the primary purpose of obtaining player eligibility or
defeating the intent of this Chapter.
Where the League Committee determines that such an arrangement exists, it may, without
limitation:
• 	refuse to recognise the ownership arrangement for League purposes;
• 	determine that the individual is not eligible to participate as a player;
• 	refuse any request for owner participation;
• 	impose sporting or disciplinary sanctions upon the franchise; or
• 	take any other action considered necessary to preserve the integrity of the
competition.
Nothing contained in this Chapter shall prevent genuine commercial or financial changes
in franchise ownership. However, where the League Committee reasonably concludes that
a change in ownership has been undertaken primarily to obtain a sporting advantage or
defeat the purpose of these Regulations, it shall be entitled to disregard such arrangement
for the purposes of these Competition Regulations.
15.6 Future Owner Participation
The League acknowledges that several franchise owners possess the playing ability to
compete at the highest level of the competition and recognises their contribution to the
continued growth of the sport.
Notwithstanding the foregoing, the League presently considers the separation of
ownership and player participation to be more closely aligned with its long-term vision for
the Centre Court Pickleball League.
Should the League Committee, after consultation with the participating franchises,
determine that owner participation would strengthen rather than compromise the League's
