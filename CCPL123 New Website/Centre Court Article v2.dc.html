Centre Court Dream returns for a second season

Centre Court Press Office
{{s.mark}}

The tournament that filled the stands last summer is back, with a wider trial window, eight franchise squads and a live player auction on 5 September.

Registration for the second season of Centre Court Dream opened this week. Players enter through the trials, are ranked, and then go into the auction pool where franchise owners build their squads for the group stage.

Season one ran across six weekends and closed with a final that sold out in under an hour. The format returns largely unchanged: a seeded single-elimination bracket, best of three sets, with the finals played over five.

What changes this season

Two additions to the calendar. Trials now run for three weeks instead of one, giving players outside the city a second window to enter. And every group-stage match will be streamed, with match data published the following morning.

Squad size is capped at twelve, with at least four places reserved for players who came through the open trials rather than direct invitation. Franchise owners spend from a fixed purse at the auction.

How to enter

Registration closes when the auction pool is locked on 4 September. Players need a valid ID, a declared age category, and confirmation of availability across the group-stage weekends.
